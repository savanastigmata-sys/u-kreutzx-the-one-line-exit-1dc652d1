Entrants write one resignation letter, exactly one line long, whose only job is to be funny. It can come from any job, any workplace, any reason for leaving, as long as it reads like a letter a person could actually send and the joke lands in that single line. Entries are compared two at a time by a judge model working from the brief published with this bounty, and the best-ranked lines win a share of the reward. Free to enter.

This entry's line (114 characters, one line):

> I hereby resign: the office plant died on my watch, and unlike it, I refuse to be watered once a week and ignored.

## Run it
